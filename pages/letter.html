💖 Letter From Home

A little piece of home to keep with you in France.

Dear Abhisha,

So you're really going to France. 🇫🇷

I hope this year gives you more memories than you could ever fit into a photo album. I hope you explore new places, meet amazing people, try things you've never tried before, and have those random little moments that you remember years later.

There will probably be days when everything feels exciting and perfect, and there might also be days when you feel tired, overwhelmed, or just miss home. And that's completely okay.

On those days, remember that you don't have to figure everything out at once. Take a deep breath, give yourself a little time, and remember how far you've already come.

While you're busy discovering France, there are people back home who are going to be thinking about you, missing you, and waiting to hear all your stories.

So take lots of pictures. Eat the croissants. Get on that random train. Find your favourite café. Get a little lost. Laugh way too much. Make mistakes. Make friends. And most importantly, enjoy being there. 🥐💙

And whenever you miss home, come back to this little corner. It's always going to be here for you.

No matter how many kilometres are between us, you're never really that far away.

With lots of love,
Sahithi 💙
